# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless
of experience level, background, or identity.

This is a learning-focused project. Questions are encouraged. There are no "stupid questions" here.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**

- Be kind and constructive in issues, discussions, and pull requests
- Welcome newcomers and help them get started
- Give and accept feedback gracefully
- Focus on what's best for the learning community

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Dismissive responses to beginner questions
- Trolling or deliberately unconstructive comments
- Publishing others' private information

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer directly. The
maintainer reserves the right to remove comments, close issues, or ban participants who violate this code of conduct.
